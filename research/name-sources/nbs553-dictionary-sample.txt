Source: Circular of the National Bureau of Standards No. 553, "The ISCC-NBS
Method of Designating Colors and a Dictionary of Color Names" (Kelly & Judd,
1955), U.S. Department of Commerce / National Bureau of Standards.

Full scan: https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-C13-65f63ba398aa703418c5e0881f2985ab
(also on archive.org and HathiTrust). 172 pp., OCR text layer by the Internet
Archive. This excerpt was produced here by `pdftotext -layout` on the govinfo
PDF, for research purposes only — it is NOT a clean re-typed dataset (OCR
has column-bleed and character errors, e.g. "l" for "1", stray source-code
fragments running into the next column). A real import would need a fresh,
careful re-OCR or manual retyping of the three-column tables, not reuse of
this raw dump.

Legal status: U.S. government work, 1955. Explicitly marked public domain —
see the companion NIST paper (Kelly, J. Res. NBS 61(5), 1958), which states
in its masthead "papers are in the public domain and are not subject to
copyright in the United States."

Each row is: Color name | Source code | ISCC-NBS designation + block number.
Source-code key (partial, from p.84 of the Circular): M = Maerz & Paul,
R = Ridgway 1912, P = Plochere, T = Textile Color Card Association,
TC = Transportation Corps US Army, F = Federal Standard / AN (Army-Navy)
aeronautical specs, H = Horticultural Colour Chart, SC/RC/A/B/S = various
standards bodies listed in the Circular's front matter.
