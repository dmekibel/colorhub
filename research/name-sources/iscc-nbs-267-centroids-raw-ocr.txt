U.S. government work, public domain. This is the OFFICIAL Munsell H V/C
notation for the centroid of each of the 267 ISCC-NBS color-name blocks,
straight from NIST, not a third-party re-digitization.

Raw text above is `pdftotext -layout` output on the NIST PDF and is NOT
clean: OCR confuses "6" and "G", "1" and "l", drops some decimal points,
and the three-column layout occasionally interleaves rows. It is kept here
only to document provenance and show the data exists and is reachable; a
real import would re-derive these 267 rows carefully (by hand, or against
a second independent transcription such as the `munsellinterpol`/`Polychrome`
R packages, which should be used only to CHECK a fresh transcription, not
copied directly, since their own derivation chain back to NIST is not
fully documented).

These 267 rows are gen tic block names ("vivid pink", "deep yellowish brown",
"black") and are useful as a VALUE SOURCE (converting each Munsell notation
to sRGB), not as app-facing vocabulary — they are the same kind of
lightness/strength compound the app already avoids teaching as primaries.
The real naming value is the ~7,500-entry synonym dictionary in NBS
Circular 553 / NBS SP 440 (see nbs553-dictionary-sample.txt), which maps
real historical/commercial names to these 267 blocks.
