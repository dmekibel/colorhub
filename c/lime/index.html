Lime or Kelly green?

A bright yellow-green, much lighter and yellower than kelly green.

Compare: Kelly green

Language

Lime green is recorded as a color name by 1883; the Oxford English Dictionary's first example is from a London newspaper in 1890.

Science

A 2009 US Fire Administration study found fluorescent yellow-green and orange the easiest colors to spot in daylight, and some fire departments now paint their trucks lime-yellow instead of red. It works because your eye is most sensitive to yellow-green (trichromacy).

Kin
