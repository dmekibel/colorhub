Ash or Silver?

Silver with a faint green-grey cast.

Compare: Silver

After wood ash.

Symbolism

On Ash Wednesday, the first day of Lent, many Western Christians are marked with ash made by burning the palms from the previous year's Palm Sunday. The traditional words come from Genesis: 'Remember that you are dust, and to dust you shall return.' See liturgical-colors.

Kin

  • Charcoal: What burning leaves behind: ash if fully burned, charcoal if not
  • Purple: Lent begins with ashes, and the Church wears violet through it

In paintings

  • The Great Wave off Kanagawa, Katsushika Hokusai
  • The Fighting Temeraire, J. M. W. Turner
