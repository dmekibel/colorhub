Cerise or Crimson?

Pinker than crimson.

Compare: Crimson

Language

Cerise is French for cherry. English borrowed it as a color in the mid-1800s: the Oxford English Dictionary's first example is from The Times in 1858, though a book of crochet patterns used it in 1845.

Kin

  • Raspberry: Another red named after a fruit
  • Ecru: Another French word English kept as a color: écru, raw
