Salmon or Coral?

Pinker and softer than coral.

Compare: Coral

Science

Salmon are pink because of what they eat. Krill and shrimp carry astaxanthin, a red-orange pigment, and it builds up in the fish's muscle. Without it, farmed salmon would be pale, so farmers add the pigment to the feed and choose the shade from a numbered color fan.

Language

Salmon has been a color name in English since 1776.

Kin

  • Coral: Two pink-oranges named after sea creatures
  • Pink: Flamingos get their pink the same way: carotenoids in their food
  • Carmine: Another animal-made color that ends up in food
