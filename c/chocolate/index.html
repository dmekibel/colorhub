Chocolate or Rust?

A deep brown, darker than rust and less red.

Compare: Rust

Language

Chocolate came into English through Spanish from Nahuatl, the language of the Aztecs. The popular story derives it from xocolatl, bitter water, but linguists dispute that, and the true source word is unsettled.

Kin

  • Carmine: Two gifts of Aztec Mexico to Europe: cacao and cochineal
  • Maroon: Two browns named after foods: cacao and chestnut
