Camel or Tan?

Darker and a little richer than tan.

Compare: Tan

After camel hair, the coat fabric.

Design

Camel hair, gathered from Bactrian camels as they shed, makes soft, warm coat cloth. Max Mara's 101801 coat, designed by Anne-Marie Beretta in 1981 in camel-colored wool and cashmere, became the label's icon.

Kin

  • Tan: Another warm brown named after what coats are made of
  • Taupe: Another color named after an animal
