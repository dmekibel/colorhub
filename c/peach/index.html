Peach or Apricot?

Paler and softer than apricot.

Compare: Apricot

Language

Peach means Persian. The Romans called it malum persicum, Persian apple, and the word wore down to peach. But the fruit comes from China, where people in the Yangtze valley were already selecting peaches by about 6000 BCE.

Design

Pantone named Peach Fuzz its Color of the Year for 2024.

Kin

  • Apricot: The early-ripening cousin of the 'Persian apple'
  • Pistachio: Another food that carries Persia in its name
  • Coral: Another Pantone Color of the Year, 2019's Living Coral
