Gunmetal or Charcoal?

Darker than charcoal.

Compare: Charcoal

After gunmetal, the bronze once cast into cannons, in its dark tarnished state.

History

Gunmetal is a bronze, about 88 percent copper with tin and zinc, first used mainly for guns and long since replaced by steel. Freshly cast it is golden, not grey; the color name matches the darker look of old metal and steel gun barrels.

Kin

  • Steel blue: Another color named after worked metal
