Mustard or Gold?

A darker, earthier yellow than gold.

Compare: Gold

Language

Mustard is named after grape juice. French moustarde comes from Latin mustum, must, the fresh grape juice that was mixed with ground seeds to make the paste.

Kin

  • Burgundy: Dijon mustard and Burgundy wine come from the same French region
  • Ochre: An earthy yellow close to painters' yellow ochre

  • Sunflowers, Vincent van Gogh
