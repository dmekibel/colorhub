Tangerine or Burnt orange?

Lighter and yellower than burnt orange.

Compare: Burnt orange

Language

Tangerine means 'of Tangier', the Moroccan port that shipped the fruit, a kind of mandarin. The word appears in 1710 for a native of Tangier; the fruit has been called a tangerine since the 1840s.

Design

Pantone named Tangerine Tango its Color of the Year for 2012.

Kin

  • Orange: Another color named after a citrus fruit
  • Magenta: Another color named, indirectly, after a place
