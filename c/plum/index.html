Plum or Mauve?

Deeper and richer than mauve.

Compare: Mauve

Language

Plum and prune are the same word. Both come from Latin prunum. English wore it down to plum for the fruit, then borrowed prune from French, where it still means a fresh plum, for the dried one.

Kin
