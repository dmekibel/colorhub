Olive or Forest green?

A yellowish dark green. Forest green is greener.

Compare: Forest green

The color of green olives.

Symbolism

In Genesis, Noah's dove comes back with an olive leaf, the sign the flood is over, and the olive branch became a sign of peace. In Greek myth, Athena won Athens from Poseidon by giving it the first olive tree.

History

Olive drab, a brownish olive, is recorded from 1892 and became the US Army's field color in the Second World War.

Design

In 2012 Australia first described the ugliest color it could find for cigarette packs, Pantone 448 C, as olive green. Olive growers objected, and the official name became drab dark brown.

Kin

  • Khaki: The two great military colors: khaki drill and olive drab
  • Brown: Australia's cigarette-pack brown was briefly called olive
  • Cornflower: Tutankhamun's wreath wove olive leaves with cornflowers
