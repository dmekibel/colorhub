Apricot or Peach?

A deeper, stronger orange than peach.

Compare: Peach

Language

Apricot and precocious share a root. Latin praecoquum meant early-ripening, because apricots ripen before peaches. The word passed into Greek, then Arabic as al-barquq, then Catalan, and reached English in the 1500s as abrecock.

Kin

  • Peach: The early-ripening cousin of the 'Persian apple'
  • Aubergine: Another food word that reached English through Arabic and Spanish
