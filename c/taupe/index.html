Taupe or Slate?

A brownish grey. Slate is a bluish grey.

Compare: Slate

Language

Taupe is French for mole, from Latin talpa. It first meant the grey-brown of a mole's fur; an English-language fashion note of 1846, printed in Australia, lists it among the fashionable greys. By the 1930s color standardizers found it had drifted far browner than real mole fur, and today it covers almost any greyish brown.

Kin
