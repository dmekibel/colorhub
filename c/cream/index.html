Cream or Ivory?

Yellower than ivory.

Compare: Ivory

After the cream that rises on fresh milk.

Design

In the 1760s Josiah Wedgwood perfected a cream-colored earthenware and supplied it to Queen Charlotte, which let him sell it as Queen's ware. Creamware was cheaper than porcelain and filled tables across Europe: by the 1780s Wedgwood shipped most of his output abroad, including the vast Frog Service for Catherine the Great.
