Puce or Mauve?

Lighter and pinker than mauve.

Compare: Mauve

Language

Puce is French for flea. Couleur puce, flea color, was the fashion at the court of Louis XVI in the late 1700s and is said to have been a favorite of Marie Antoinette.

History

In the summer of 1775, the story goes, Louis XVI saw Marie Antoinette in a brownish silk gown and called it couleur de puce, flea color. The court copied it at once, and merchants sold a whole family of fleas: young flea, old flea, flea's belly, flea's back and flea's thigh.

Kin
