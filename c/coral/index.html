Coral or Salmon?

More orange and more vivid than salmon.

Compare: Salmon

After red coral, worn as jewelry for thousands of years.

History

Red coral, Corallium rubrum, is the skeleton of tiny Mediterranean sea animals. It has been carved into beads and charms since antiquity, and European babies were long given coral amulets and teething rattles for luck and protection. Coral is recorded as a color in English from 1513.

Poetry

Ovid tells where coral came from. In the Metamorphoses, Perseus lays Medusa's severed head on a bed of seaweed, and the soft weed hardens to stone as it touches her.

Design

Pantone chose Living Coral as its Color of the Year for 2019.
