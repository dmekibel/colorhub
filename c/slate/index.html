Slate or Charcoal?

A mid grey with a blue tint, lighter than charcoal.

Compare: Charcoal

After slate rock, split into roof tiles.

Science

Slate is mudstone squeezed by heat and pressure until it splits into thin, flat sheets, perfect for roofs. Schoolchildren once wrote on handheld slates and wiped them clean, which is where a clean slate comes from.
