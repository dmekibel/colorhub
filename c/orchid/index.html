Orchid or Magenta?

Softer than magenta.

Compare: Magenta

Language

Orchid comes from Greek orkhis, testicle, after the paired round tubers on many orchids' roots.

Design

Pantone made Radiant Orchid its Color of the Year for 2014.

Kin

  • Emerald: Pantone's Color of the Year just before it, in 2013
  • Lavender: Another purple named after a flower

Sources

  • Etymonline, 'orchid'
