The journey

Mockups for design/JOURNEY.md: the path, the first run, a mixed lesson (6 steps, 3 of them world steps, plus 2 game steps), right and wrong, the lesson-complete moment, the Cabinet, and the testing layer. Every color, painting, line and number is real data from the repo (data/core-names.json, paintings.js, poems/, fashion.js, wiki-colors.js) in the draft stage order from tools/journey_coverage.py. Tokens and archetypes follow DESIGN-SYSTEM.md: booth grey wherever a color is judged, warm black for reading, one paper primary per screen, opaque controls. The switches, the answer pills and the path's stones respond to taps. Add ?h=667 to the address to see each phone at iPhone SE height.

The pathOne column of stones in their own colors, chapters as bands, one glowing next thing. Locked chapters are named, never hidden.
Steps, mid-lessonEach step takes 10 seconds or less and asks one thing. Meet, then pick, then recall. World steps put the same name on real surfaces: a poem line, a painting, a decade's palette. Two game steps come from the §20 grammar.
Right and wrongFeedback comes only after the attempt is finished. It names what it was, and when you miss, it says one thing about your answer. Rings go outside the swatch, so you can keep comparing.
Rewards that tell the truthThe delight lives in the moments: a fan of the colors you met, real pieces flying into your Cabinet. The one number that claims knowledge, Yours, only moves after a night.
Knowing what you knowPlacement (03), checkpoints, jump here, seals, personal reviews and the stage exam. None of them use hints or hearts. Only answers given a night or more after you learned a color can make it yours.
Screenshots of every frame are in design/journey-shots/ (one PNG per phone, 375 × 812 at 2x).
