The signature: a bubble becomes its pageEvery color in ColorHub opens the same way: honeycomb bubbles, palette chips, near-name bubbles, swatches and the four rooms. The tapped shape is the page; it never cuts to a new screen. Opening takes 420 ms on cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1). Closing is the same path reversed in 340 ms, and the pull-down gesture tracks the finger.
