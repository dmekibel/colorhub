# Design audit, 2026-10-08 (lane L12)

I screenshotted 35 screens at 375 × 812 and 320 × 568 through `tools/_qa/frame.html`, a true-width iframe driven by `tools/_qa/frameshots.sh`. The before/after contact sheet is `design/polish-before-after.png`.

Flaws are ranked by how much they hurt the "iconic, expensive, never overwhelming" bar. ✅ = fixed in this pass, → = handed to the owning lane.

## P0: breaks the system or the screen
1. ✅ **The Rooms corner covered content at the bottom-left of every room.**
   - Examples: "Purples", the Train eye rows ("…N READY") and Studio's "Harmony" label.
   - Fix: each room sheet now fades into `--surface-1` over its last ~104 px, and its scroll end reserves corner + inset + 64 px, so the last line clears the corner.
2. ✅ **Train and Studio rendered the old brand row inside the Room sheet.**
   - The brand row was "ColorHub" + ⋯ + a 68 px tab title.
   - Now they get the Room header: `title-1` on the left and a `note` on the right ("Sharpen your eye", "Make your own").
   - `roomChrome()` converts any legacy `tabHead()` + `.tab-title` pair, so other lanes' JS doesn't change.
3. ✅ **At 320 × 568 the stem ran off the top of the screen** (Studio's bubble was cut in half).
   - The bubble spacing and size now scale to the screen height (`--n`, `--step`), with smaller labels under 600 px tall.
4. ✅ **Tracked uppercase mono labels were everywhere, about 130 rules.**
   - Examples: "19 ROUNDS · ABOUT 3 MINUTES", "SCREEN NOT CHECKED", "AGAIN / GOT IT", "REDS & PINKS", "FOUR CLOSE SHADES", "SWIPE UP TO MEET THEM", "POINT AT ANYTHING", "DRAG THE SHAPE…", "STRIPES/BY AREA", "NEW / not tried yet", "STAGE / SHOW / COLLECTIONS", "LOOK-ALIKES ↗", and the taste wheel's hue names.
   - All of them are now serif italic notes. Mono stays only on hex values, percentages and counts.
   - Files L12 owns were edited in place. The lanes still editing gym, match, gallery and honey get overrides in `css/polish.css`, so their files stay conflict-free.

## P1: inconsistency that reads as cheap
5. ✅ **The radius scale wasn't unified:** 1, 2, 3, 8, 10, 12, 18, 19, 20, 22, 24 and 30 px all appeared. 95 declarations now snap to `--r-1`, `--r-2`, `--r-3` or `--r-pill`.
6. ✅ **Outline-only controls:**
   - View's Show/Look tabs, every `.seg` control (palette 3/6/10, Stripes/By area/Chips, the gallery's 3/6/12/20) and the Taste/Chicago `glass-pill`.
   - They are now one solid `surface-2` track with the chosen option in paper.
7. ✅ **Glass (backdrop blur) on controls:** the camera hint, the gallery badge, Explore search, the poem view toggle and the honeycomb `glass-box` and caption. All are solid level-2 surfaces now.
8. ✅ **AA contrast on the deck's paper label:** "About scarlet" and "look-alikes ↗" were `--soft` on paper (about 2.3:1). They're now `--paper-soft` (5.8:1).
9. ✅ **Empty states:**
   - Studio's "Your photos" showed a mono "Loading…" or a sans paragraph under its heading, and "Your palettes" showed a sans paragraph.
   - Both are now one quiet serif note line, per DESIGN-SYSTEM §10.
10. ✅ **Two different press dips (.92 and .9 with a spring overshoot)** and many tiles with no pressed state at all. There's now one press: scale .97 for tiles and cards, opacity .6 for rows, .94 for corners and bubbles, all at `--dur-0`.

## P2: motion (DESIGN-SYSTEM §8)
11. ✅ **Screens and sheets used three easings (.5 s ease, .4 s ease, .25 s).** Screens now rise and sheets slide on the signature curve (`--grow`, `--ease-grow`), and the scrim fades on `--dur-2`. Reduce Motion turns each of these into a 200 ms fade.
12. ✅ **The bubble-to-page grow happened only for hand-marked chips.** Every `[data-swatch]` in the app now flies its chip into the color page it opens (`MORPH_TRIGGER` in core.js).
13. ✅ **There was no lesson-complete moment.** Now:
    - Learn it's plates (and a unit's chips) rise one by one;
    - the title and rows follow;
    - one "done" haptic fires (10·30·20).
14. ✅ **Fly to the map (genius panel):** `flyToMap(colors, sourceEls)` in `js/polish.js`.
    - Home takes over and each new color flies to its own bubble, located by the honeycomb's new `ctrl.locate(hex)`.
    - A paper ring lights the bubble on landing, then a "N new on your map" toast shows.
    - The Journey lane calls it.

## New shared components (genius panel)
- **The relation mark** (`.rel[data-s]`, `relMark(c)`, auto-applied as `[data-rel]` to every `[data-swatch]`, look-alike row, near-name chip and honeycomb caption):
  - nothing = unmet;
  - an outline ring = met;
  - a solid disc = Yours;
  - a half disc = you mix it up.
  - `.rel-thread` joins a confused pair, and `.rel-key` is the legend.
  - It reads `lmStatus()` when the Learner Model provides it, otherwise `S.cards`.
  - It's never drawn on booth-grey judged screens.
- **The squint key** (`[data-sq-key]`, `squintKey()`): a solid 44 px button, bottom-right on every painting hero, photo palette and art image.
  - Images go through a per-pixel L* pass (CSS `grayscale()` is the fallback for images without CORS), and swatches turn to the grey of the same L*.
  - It's one global toggle, and turning it off restores everything.
