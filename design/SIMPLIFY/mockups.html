One map, four places

A clickable sketch of the navigation in design/SIMPLIFY/PLAN.md. Everything is real Night Gallery tokens at 440 × 956; the content is sample data.

Try this path

  1. Tap a bubble: its page opens.
  2. Tap a painting on that page, then a color in the painting.
  3. ‹ walks back one step and says where it goes. Hold it to see the whole trail.
  4. The pill on the right (Map, Museum…) closes the pages and takes you back to the place you started from. A pull down from the top does the same.
  5. ≡ Places (bottom left) lists the places and every collection, plus "Pick up where you left off".
  6. ⋯ on any screen: search first, then that screen's options and tools, then Settings.
  7. Every collection (paintings, photographs, pulp covers, fashion, gems, poems…) is two taps from any place: ≡ Places, then its tile. Recent ones come first.
  8. In search, try "pulp", "slideshow", "eyedropper" or "haptics": collections and features are findable too.
