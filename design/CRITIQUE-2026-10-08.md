## Top fixes (all done in this pass)
1. **Deep links to late-loading screens** (`js/boot.js`, `js/router.js`). artwiki.js, article.js, looks.js and fashion.js load after boot.js. So a first load of `#/painter/…`, `#/arthistory`, `#/movement/…` and the like fell back to Home. Those screens also never got their own address (the URL stayed `#/today` and the title stayed "Learn"). Boot now starts on DOMContentLoaded, and router.js wraps the late screens on the same event. index.html is untouched.
2. **Painter timeline honesty** (`js/artwiki.js` awYearOk/awDecOk). Placeholder years (1500) and blank years are left out of the axis, the decade columns, the lightness line and the change-point sentence. Decades are written in full ("1870s"), and the numbers are rounded.
3. **No uppercase mono for words** (`css/artwiki.css`, end of file). Every word label in the art wiki and the painting Analysis now uses the serif note voice. Mono stays only on numbers.
4. **Fashion list rows** (`css/world.css`, end of file). Palette on the left, title over dek on the right.
5. **Copy and small layout:** "is yours" (with the right singular or plural); the analysis sentence and its archive size; the movement caveat collapsed to one calm line with the details a tap away; taste-profile primary, Warm/Cool label and painter label; measured differences on "Which" pages; the rooms note for Learn; the relation mark on a row's chip (`js/polish.js`); counts formatted ("1,143"); broken painting photos hide instead of showing the browser's broken-image icon.

## Follow-ups (bigger than a safe CSS/copy fix)
- **Hub pages need a design.** Plates ordered light to dark or by sub-family, a one-line intro, the core words first and visibly separate, consistent casing, and no joke names (the xkcd "Ugly Blue"). Today a hub is a raw list of 208 rows.
- **Naming policy in palettes.** Look palettes and garment chips name colors by the nearest of about 2,700 names, which picks Japanese traditional names ("Kenpōzome", "Rikancha", "Hihada-iro", "Kokushoku") and web-codes ("Khaki (Web)") as primary names. CLAUDE.md says these are secondary. The nearest-name pass for palettes should prefer the Learn layer.
- **Art history index is monotonous.** About 70 rows of nearly identical rainbow bars bury the signal. Consider showing only what stands out per decade (the bars' deviation), or a century summary that opens to decades.
- **Duplicate finding on the painting page.** "Darker than 94%…" appears in the Analysis tiles and again in Findings. Its n comes from the analysis build, while the tiles had a hard-coded size. One source should own the archive count.
- **Squint state when scrolled.** Once the image is off screen nothing says the page is value-only. A small fixed "Squint on" pill, or keeping the key in the header, would fix it.
- **Rooms stem art.** Train and Studio both show a conic rainbow. Train should show today's station tile (DESIGN-SYSTEM §2).
- **Ten drops' disabled primary** ("Spend 10 more") is low contrast on booth grey. Consider an outline-free quiet text until it's ready.
- **Painter "rooms" strip.** Its first chip is often near-black on black with no hairline (DESIGN-SYSTEM §3 asks for a 1 px inner hairline below L* 12).
