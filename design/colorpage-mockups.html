The color page

Mockups for design/COLOR-PAGE-DESIGN.md: one page engine for every one of the ~2,700 archive colors, with the article as a book you open from it. Three cases (a color with a long story, a niche color that borrows its near twin's story, a tapped in-between color), the reading mode, the notes, the Compass, the trail, the states, a hub and a Versus page. Every color, painting, count and ΔE is computed from the repo's real data (data/library.json, data/core-names.json, the 23,531-painting gallery pools, CIEDE2000); article prose and article metadata (chapter titles, minutes, source counts) are draft copy for layout, written from facts already in the repo or from well-established history, and L7 will rewrite them from fact cards; Learner Model lines ("Yours since 3 Oct", "seen twice") are illustrative. Tokens and archetypes follow DESIGN-SYSTEM.md: warm black for reading, opaque controls, one paper primary per screen, mono only on numbers and codes. The yellow notes are annotations, not UI.

A color with a long story: Madder lakeThe cover teaches before you scroll: name, where the name comes from, a sourced one-line definition, and the most surprising facts peeking at the bottom. The story is entered from its contents, field notes stay grouped, and the page ends in your world.
The reading modeA book you open from the page: warm black, a long measure in Geist, the color as a ribbon that fills as you read (never as text), notes in a sheet, contents one tap away. Closing it shrinks the book back into the card you opened it from.
Moving between colorsWalking color space by name is the core pleasure. Tap grows a color into its page; holding the hero raises the Compass under your thumb; long-pressing ‹ shows your trail; Back unwinds to the map, which draws the walk you took.
A niche color borrows depth, honestlyPearl Night Blue is an industrial finish (RAL 5026) with no story of its own. Its nearest color with a long story is Prussian blue, 96% match. The page says so in the color's own terms, shows the twin's opening lines, says exactly how the two differ, and opens the story in one tap.
StatesA very dark pigment that's in your lessons, a vivid color no painting reaches, a crowd word with almost no history, and a very light color still loading. No state hides absence: when the archive is silent, the silence is the finding.
A hub and a Versus pageHubs and Versus pages are the same query engine rendered two ways. A hub is a computed exhibition with a walk through it; a Versus page answers "teal or turquoise?", which is what people actually search for.
