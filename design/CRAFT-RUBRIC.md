# Craft rubric (fresh-context critic; score 1–5 per line; anything under 4 gets a concrete fix)

1. **First 10 seconds:** I understand what to do without reading. The rules are taught by doing.
2. **Depth:** there's a real skill to master. It's not trivially easy, not random, and not one-note after 3 rounds.
3. **Curve:** difficulty rises and breathes; new twists arrive at easy difficulty; a miss eases the next round.
4. **Feedback:** every tap answers with anticipation, impact and settle (the visual, a haptic, the words). Right and wrong feel distinct and kind. A wrong answer teaches something (it shows the true color and the direction words).
5. **States:** first time, empty, loading, error, win, lose, streak, level up, mastery, end of session, and returning tomorrow. All designed, none default.
6. **Meaning:** the result is honest (real thresholds, % different, n) and connects outward (the Learner Model, color pages, the map, Practice).
7. **Replay:** bests, stars, a daily seed, a shareable result, and a reason to come back.
8. **Clean:** one primary action; no clutter; DESIGN-SYSTEM type, spacing and tokens; opaque controls; no glass; mono only for codes.
9. **Polish:** no placeholder copy, emoji grids or uppercase-mono shouting; good at 320 px; very light and very dark colors legible; numbers formatted.
10. **Delight:** at least one moment that makes you smile, earned rather than decorative.

Output: a table of scores, then the top 5 fixes (specific: file, element, change), then a verdict: SHIP / REVISE.
